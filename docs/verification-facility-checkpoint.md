# Facility and vehicle visual checkpoint — independent WebGL2

This audit used a separate headless Chrome at 1600 × 900 against the frozen production server on port 4176. The scripts operate Play, WASD/E, the creative panel, the map and mouse controls. `?test` permits read-only diagnostics; no game objects were mutated to make a normal-control assertion pass. Map fast travel and clearing ammunition are explicit sandbox actions. Other GPU work occurred concurrently, so the FPS readings are not a performance gate.

## Evidence and observations

- `tests/facility-visual-audit.mjs` and `docs/evidence/facility-checkpoint-3-webgl*.json/png` preserve the startup failure, first retest and captured-pointer run separately. The initial build `index-BWJ1hGx5.js` failed before welcome with native `fetch` “Illegal invocation.” The subsequent build starts normally. The first retest's camera-orbit assertion failed because the browser had not captured the pointer; an ordinary canvas click enabled mouse look in the next run.
- Normal E entry opened a coupe door to 0.95 radians during transition, then attached Jason to `vehicle-1` and closed the door. E exit worked. L toggled headlight state and the side-view screenshots show illuminated lamps with lights on. The night scene changes sky/windows and lamp appearance, but the buildings remain unusually bright and flat. The seated side view visibly shows feet below the chassis; this is a visual defect, despite successful mounting. Root is addressing the seat pose.
- Normal map travel reached the annex entrance at (−449, 144). Walking west stopped at X −455.475 against the physical closed boom. E granted a visitor pass; the boom raised and a subsequent W walk reached X −463.615, Z 143.974 inside the perimeter, with zero wanted stars.
- All four stable military actors activated. Three changed positions on their local patrol routes; one remained at the gate. Independently viewed screenshots show olive headwear, clothing/vest and markings, but blue trousers remain from the common character. The firearm is not clearly visible in the captured frontal aim views; this is not sufficient evidence to claim a finished weapon/grip visual.
- Holding RMB inside revoked access, showed the six-second warning, then raised the alarm. Wanted state first entered its legitimate reporting delay and subsequently reached three-star pursuit. Guards approached and aimed. The last assertion incorrectly expected an instantaneous `state === 'firing'`: the implementation sets that state only on a shot frame, so a single snapshot cannot establish whether shots occurred. Invulnerability was enabled and ammunition had been cleared. This run therefore does **not** certify actual received guard damage or visible muzzle flashes.

The captured-pointer run has no runtime exception, console error or failed HTTP request. Its one error is the overly narrow shot-state assertion. Earlier failed runs remain in the evidence rather than being overwritten.

## Rendering defect under investigation

At the annex, most static scenery disappears against the sky: terrain, buildings and fence are absent, while the boom, signs and characters still render. The defect is visible in `facility-checkpoint-3-webgl-captured-closed-gate.png`, `-inside-guard.png` and `-guard-response.png`. Collision and streamed-package readiness continue to work. A second fresh run with no mouse look and no vehicle interaction reproduces the missing scenery after normal map travel, ruling out the audit orbit as its cause.

`tests/world-material-audit.mjs` and `docs/evidence/facility-checkpoint-3-materials*.json/png` record loaded material and mesh diagnostics. The world materials are PBRMaterial instances, with `disableLighting=false`, `unlit=false`, alpha 1 and zero stucco emissive color. They remain registered in the scene. Representative stucco has roughness 0.89 and environment intensity 0.7. Shared-material disposal was considered but is not supported by these observations.

The fresh facility camera is at (−449, 2.524, 139.016), looking toward (−449, 1.674, 144), pitch 0.08; it is above the ground. The compound concrete mesh is enabled, visible, active and material-ready, with world bounds (−548, 0, 78) to (−456, 0.12, 198). The world owner subsequently traced the defect to shared box-template geometry being mutated during mesh merging: the 24-vertex concrete mesh has indices as high as 29, and 324 exported records contain out-of-range indices. An exporter fix and regeneration are in progress; the facility's functional collision results do not constitute a pass for its current appearance.
