# Reference register

Last inspected: **2026-09-09**. This is a research register, not a claim of GTA VI parity. Project defaults are authored choices. Implementation and visual acceptance must be recorded separately in [feature-index.md](feature-index.md).

## Evidence rules

- **Confirmed GTA VI:** Rockstar explicitly identifies an item or behavior in its current material. Confirmation of an item's existence does not confirm that it is player usable.
- **Observed GTA VI:** directly inspected official image or footage; describe only what is visible. A screenshot caption is recorded as caption evidence, not independent visual inspection.
- **GTA V fallback:** a documented GTA V behavior intentionally chosen where VI evidence is unavailable. Source confidence remains separate from this classification.
- **Inferred reconstruction:** an authored interpretation; includes unpublished dimensions, tuning and connections.
- **Creative-mode addition:** explicitly requested sandbox functionality without a claim that Rockstar provides it.

Official screenshots and in-engine cinematics can guide appearance. They do not establish input mappings, availability of interiors, handling, destruction, AI logic, or interaction rules. No leaked material is part of this register.

## Geography observations

VI-PLACES describes Ocean Beach's Art Deco hotels and sand, Little Cuba bakeries, Tisha-Wocka market, and VC Port. The Keys are an archipelago; Grassrivers has mangroves and alligators; Port Gellhorn has worn tourist commerce; Ambrosia has the Allied Crystal refinery; Mount Kalaga is on the northern border. These brief descriptions establish regional character, not a world map.

The Vice City postcard was visually inspected: turquoise water, vegetated islands, boats and a tower skyline. The Mount Kalaga postcard was visually inspected: wooded slopes, exposed rock, a watercourse, and atmospheric depth. Other region image descriptions were read through the official accessibility captions. None were measured photogrammetrically. Google Maps / Street View measurements and detailed image comparisons remain an explicit research task.

## Wanted-profile decision

**Do not declare five stars to be confirmed GTA VI.** Current secondary coverage reports six, and current primary footage exists but was inaccessible for frame inspection here. Keep `maxWantedLevel`, thresholds, responder composition and identification weights data driven. A first playable five-level profile may be labeled `project-gta-v-inspired`; its numeric timers are project choices, not verified V or VI data. A six-level `vi-reported-experimental` profile can be introduced without presenting it as primary-verified fidelity.

Militarized pursuit is a creative addition until supported directly. Restricted military access is independently specified in the feature index. A sixth star must not be equated with military arrival without evidence.
