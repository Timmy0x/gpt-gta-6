# Shared body-region injury state and poses

`Injuries.ts` keeps left/right arms, left/right legs, head and torso damage independently. `Character.bodyInjuries` is separate from the legacy casualty marker, so a walking survivor retains partial injury without becoming a permanent ragdoll. Regional trauma receives local damage before health multipliers. Projectile health damage uses head ×2, torso ×1 and limbs ×0.65; melee head damage uses ×1.35. These are authored game rules, not medical or Rockstar specifications.

`applyBodyImpact` creates a fresh state. Healing uses simulation time and independent regional delays: 35 seconds for minor injuries, 90 for serious/projectile injuries, and 180 for severe injuries, followed by severity reduction at 1/300 per second. A hit leaving health at or below 20 records separate systemic impairment. Pausing, character switching and save/load do not advance these timers.

Effects supply movement mode, speed scale/cap, sprint/jump/stand/aim permissions, weapon-handling scale, regional severities and the dominant limp side. Severe regional or systemic injury causes a physical fall followed by crawling. Separate recovery thresholds prevent toggling between crawling and standing. Recovery has a full three-second rise and preserves remaining partial impairment.

`Character.applyInjuryPose` follows ordinary locomotion. It shortens the injured stride, transfers support to the opposite side, guards an injured arm and hunches torso/systemic injury. The critical crawl keeps the trunk low, reaches with one arm and advances the opposite knee outward. Prone ankles use **+0.25 rad local plantarflexion**, allowing the toes to trail behind the shins; forcing shoes level previously produced an implausible −1.47 rad bend and a corrective impulse when native physics resumed. `CharacterSkinSupport` evaluates cached indexed skin geometry during prone lowering and recovery. `keepSkinAboveFloor` lifts only the authored pelvis when the visible outfit would cross the floor, then the pose replants the hands. The overlay preserves limb lengths and controller/root ownership.

`Character.applySeatedInjuryPose` keeps injured occupants in their seats while the supported upper body guards or slumps. `withSupportedPose` permits an explicit synchronous external-support animation without changing the actor's dead flag or injury state. Ordinary dead-body animation remains blocked outside that scope; active physical ragdolls cannot be overridden. Native fall fitting can synchronize the visual skin explicitly through `syncVisualPose` before sampling a freshly restored pose.

## Verification and evidence

`tests/body-injuries.test.ts` covers independent regions, health scaling, movement/aim restrictions, systemic damage, gradual recovery, validation, immutable damage snapshots, partitioned elapsed time and repeated impacts. `tests/injury-pose.test.ts` inspects actual indexed skinned geometry for Jason, Lucia and two licensed civilian bodies. It checks consecutive 60 Hz fall/crawl frames, all 180 frames of the rise, twelve idle phases for each severe body region, opposite limp direction and guarded torso posture.

The R8 run evaluates **18,771,616 indexed vertices**. Minimum visible height is **+0.00600 m**, maximum standing extent **1.84789 m**, maximum moving hand/finger gap **0.05355 m**, maximum planted hand/finger gap **0.01306 m**, and maximum foot/toe gap **0.08780 m**. Crucially, the trunk or legs remain within **0.02380 m** of the floor in every crawl frame; this measure excludes hands and feet, so low fingertips cannot conceal a hovering body. Maximum consecutive crawl-joint movement is **0.06435 m per 60 Hz frame**. These are lowest visible surface measurements, not proof of a full flat palm contact patch. Non-root bone lengths remain unchanged.

The skin diagnostic now resolves imported bone names by their explicit skin index (`Bone.getIndex()`), rather than assuming skeleton-array order. This fixes misleading labels on the long civilian skirt; the numerical skinned positions and minimum-height calculations were already independent of those labels.

`tests/character-assets.test.ts` and `tests/characters.test.ts` check all visible geometry against the existing low/reclined cabin floor and roof. Critical seated overlays preserve the pelvis and leg transforms. `tests/vehicle-interaction-pose.test.ts` checks contact IK lifetime and explicit supported-pose ownership.

The offline studio is `scripts/characters/injuries/`. R8 captures **720 consecutive side-view frames**, plus 28 selected overview frames and twelve closeups, with zero browser errors or warnings. The source fingerprint, exact test log and selected images are in `docs/evidence/body-injury-poses-r8`; dense side frames are in the ignored `.local-builds/body-injury-r8-frames` review directory. R6 and R7 were rejected after side views showed unsupported trunk/leg hover despite small fingertip gaps. Combined native physical fall/handoff validation remains a separate acceptance gate.

## Limits

The authored studio covers a flat ground plane. It does not establish normal-input hit routing, ragdoll constraints, save/load, driver extraction, or full gameplay behavior; those have separate native and integrated audits. Individual limbs still need terrain-specific sampling on uneven ground. Fingers are not independently articulated into a flat contact patch, and loose clothing has no full cloth simulation. The licensed models are generic substitutes, not exact Jason/Lucia likenesses. No GTA V/VI animation or graphics parity is claimed.
