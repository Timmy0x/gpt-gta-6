# Aerometrex Miami partner access check

Checked 12 September 2026. No authorized, publicly downloadable Aerometrex Miami/Brickell package was established through these routes. Public viewing or an empty license field is not a grant to download and reuse the mesh. No model payload, private viewer API data or tiles were acquired; no account, form submission, purchase or vendor contact was made.

## Cesium

The official [Aerometrex partnership announcement](https://cesium.com/blog/2021/04/06/use-aerometrex-reality-mesh-models-in-cesium-for-unreal/) demonstrates Miami, while its named evaluation dataset is Denver. Aerometrex's own [Unreal integration article](https://aerometrex.com.au/resources/blog/cesium-unreal-making-aerometrex-data-ready-for-interactivity/) makes the same distinction. Neither establishes a downloadable Miami package.

Cesium's current [Archives and Exports documentation](https://cesium.com/learn/ion/cesium-ion-archives-and-exports/) limits archive/export to data uploaded by the account holder; Asset Depot content cannot be downloaded or exported through that feature. No account was created and no Asset Depot token or tile payload was inspected.

## Official free-sample route

The [official sample-data page](https://aerometrex.com/models/sample-data/) and [World Leaders page](https://aerometrex.com/world-leaders-in-3d/) lead to the same [sample request form](https://go.aerometrex.com/l/1060902/2024-05-16/pnvz3m). The form explicitly describes **Denver** samples at 2 cm, 5 cm and street-level resolution in OBJ, FBX, 3D Tiles or SLPK. It requests contact details and includes email-communications consent. It does not offer a self-service Miami package. The form was read only.

## Sketchfab

A read-only [public model search for Aerometrex](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/search?type=models&q=aerometrex) returned four models and no further page: Flinders Street Station, Onkaparinga Hills, Blanche Point Coastline (Segmented), and Acquedotto di Oristano. None is Miami. One unrelated model was marked downloadable with CC Attribution; that license says nothing about Aerometrex's Miami product. No models were downloaded.

These checks establish the scope of the searched routes, not a claim that no other authorized delivery arrangement can exist. The product delivery remains unresolved; the prepared coordinate adapter must not be represented as an acquired or georeferenced Miami model.
