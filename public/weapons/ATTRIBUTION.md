# Weapon model sources

The pistol, carbine and sniper meshes adapt M1911, M4/AR-15 and Dragunov/SVD by **Tabasco** from [Various Small Arms](https://opengameart.org/content/various-small-arms-assault-rifles-sniper-pistol), originally posted 2 July 2010. The author added CC0/public-domain permission on 15 December 2023. License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Changes: SI scale and hand origin, coordinate conversion, original component vertex-group separation, small machined-edge bevels, PBR material conversion, hard-edge normals, welded indices, gzip packaging and authored moving slide/bolt/magazine behavior. Source textures were not provided; these models currently use material colors, not photorealistic texture sets. Source animations are not replayed; mechanism movement is authored for this game.

Source/download hashes and complete conversion settings are in `data/weapons/tabasco` and `scripts/weapons`. The three packages total 449,487 compressed bytes. Other current weapon geometry and all handling/animation/UI are original authored work in progress. This is not Rockstar art or an exact GTA weapon inventory.
